How the course is graded

From Lecture 0. Grades are out of 20. Extra credit for participation is up to 5%.

ActivityShare
Project20%
Homework30%
Midterm20%
Final30%

Meetings on the course site are Saturdays and Mondays, 15:00–16:30, in person. Each homework may use at most 3 late days, and the term has 10 late days in total. Past that, the grade is multiplied by 0.95 per extra day. Solutions are released 3 days after the deadline, and the homework is not accepted after that.
