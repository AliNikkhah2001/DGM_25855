References and slide index

The lectures are the primary source. The books and papers below are the ones named on Lecture 0, slide 12, on Lecture 5, slide 84, and in the homework sheets.

  • Sajjad Amini, Deep Generative Models lecture decks. Lecture 2, slides 12–42, for densities, expectation, Monte Carlo, and KL. Lecture 3 for autoregressive models and MADE. Lecture 4, slides 12–40, 56–64, and 77–84, for the ELBO and the reparameterization trick. Lecture 5, slides 3–73, for change of variables, NICE, RealNVP, and MAF.

  • Kevin P. Murphy, Probabilistic Machine Learning: Advanced Topics, MIT Press, 2023. Chapter 10 for variational inference, Chapter 11 for Monte Carlo and importance sampling, Chapter 21 for variational autoencoders, Chapter 23 for normalizing flows.

  • Jakub M. Tomczak, Deep Generative Modeling, Springer. Section 4.1.2 for the change of variables, §4.1.3 for RealNVP and dequantization, §5.3 for the VAE, §5.3.6 for posterior collapse, §5.4.1.4 for a mixture prior, §5.4.1.8 for a flow prior, §5.5.2.1 for a two-level hierarchical VAE.

  • Christopher M. Bishop and Hugh Bishop, Deep Learning: Foundations and Concepts, Springer, 2023. The third book listed on the logistics slide.

  • D. P. Kingma and M. Welling, Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes, ICLR 2014.

  • D. P. Kingma and M. Welling, An Introduction to Variational Autoencoders, Foundations and Trends in Machine Learning, 2019.

  • K. Sohn, H. Lee, and X. Yan, Learning Structured Output Representation using Deep Conditional Generative Models, NeurIPS 2015.

  • C. K. Sønderby, T. Raiko, L. Maaløe, S. K. Sønderby, and O. Winther, Ladder Variational Autoencoders, NeurIPS 2016.

  • D. J. Rezende and S. Mohamed, Variational Inference with Normalizing Flows, ICML 2015.

  • L. Dinh, D. Krueger, and Y. Bengio, NICE: Non-linear Independent Components Estimation, 2015.

  • L. Dinh, J. Sohl-Dickstein, and S. Bengio, Density Estimation using Real NVP, ICLR 2017.

  • G. Papamakarios, T. Pavlakou, and I. Murray, Masked Autoregressive Flow for Density Estimation, NeurIPS 2017.

  • D. P. Kingma and P. Dhariwal, Glow: Generative Flow with Invertible \(1\times 1\) Convolutions, NeurIPS 2018.

  • J. Ho, X. Chen, A. Srinivas, Y. Duan, and P. Abbeel, Flow++: Improving Flow-Based Generative Models with Variational Dequantization and Architecture Design, ICML 2019.

  • M. Germain, K. Gregor, I. Murray, and H. Larochelle, MADE: Masked Autoencoder for Distribution Estimation, ICML 2015.

Intuition: A path through the two homeworks

Read Chapters Probability–Divergences once. Do Homework 1's Gaussian and divergence problems from Chapters The Gaussian family and Monte Carlo, and the autoregressive problems from Chapter Autoregressive models. Then read Chapter Variational autoencoders and write the conditional ELBO before you train the VAE notebook. Read Chapter Hierarchical latents before problem 2. Read the coupling section of Chapter Normalizing flows before problems 3, 4, and 5, and before the flow notebook. Do the notebook's color-transfer section first. It is the two-dimensional MAF.
